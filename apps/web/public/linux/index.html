Cryptographic Trust Pipeline & Implementation
How HimVote Mathematical Verification & Member Voting Works
HimVote completely replaces administrator trust with 4 independent, mathematically provable layers. Every vote for real candidates (President, VP, GenSec) is cast secretly, logged without tampering, and provable offline with zero knowledge of identity.
6-Stage Independent Trust Boundary Architecture
● RFC 9474 & RFC 9162 COMPLIANT
TIER 01: ELIGIBILITY & UNLINKABLE CREDENTIALS
RFC 9474 RSA Blind Signature Tokens
Students authenticate via SSO / OTP against the frozen college roster. Voter client blinds a random secret token (r) and sends the blinded envelope to the Identity Issuer. The issuer signs that the voter is eligible, without seeing the secret token or ever knowing who is voted for. Unblinded credential gives 100% unlinkability.
TIER 02: BALLOT ENCRYPTION & ZERO KNOWLEDGE
Exponential ElGamal + CDS 1-out-of-k Proofs
When a student selects a candidate (e.g., Suzi or Priya Patel), the browser client encrypts the selection using the election public key into ciphertext (c1, c2). A Chaum-Pedersen / CDS zero-knowledge proof proves mathematically that the ballot is a valid vote (0 or 1) without disclosing the choice.
TIER 03: TAMPER-EVIDENT MERKLE LOGGING
RFC 8785 Canonical JCS & RFC 9162 Trees
Every cast ballot is canonicalized per RFC 8785 (JCS) and inserted into an append-only RFC 9162 SHA-256 Merkle tree. A deterministic nullifier prevents double-voting. The student receives a cryptographic receipt handle (e.g., HIM-REC-9821-X4) carrying their leaf inclusion proof path.
TIER 04: WITNESS CO-SIGNING & ETHEREUM ANCHOR
Ed25519 Checkpoints & HimVoteAnchor.sol
Independent witness service nodes verify monotonic sequence numbers and co-sign tree heads with Ed25519 signatures. Checkpoints are anchored on an EVM smart contract. Even a rogue university database administrator cannot rewrite, erase, or fabricate ballots after anchoring.

Institutional Trust Model
Security Guarantees & Cryptographic Limits
In strict compliance with Specification Rule 0.1(7), HimVote never makes unqualified claims such as 'unhackable' or '100% secure'. Below are the exact verified mathematical properties and assumptions.
What Independent Verification Proves
• Cast-as-Intended: The voting booth encrypts the ballot using the election public key before submission.
• Recorded-as-Cast: The Merkle inclusion proof mathematically guarantees the voter's encrypted ballot exists unaltered in the log.
• Tallied-as-Recorded: Universal verifiability allows any auditor to recompute candidate vote counts from the published archive.
• Monotonic Continuity: External witness co-signatures detect if an election database attempts to rewind or erase votes.
