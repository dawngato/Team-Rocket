## Evidence boundary

The current evidence supports a substantial synthetic local prototype and several tested local integrity controls. It does not support a claim that Q7 is complete. In particular, the following remain unresolved: real external election-engine integration, protocol-native encrypted tally proof, real web/API end-to-end casting, independent witness custody, an executed Avalonia desktop build, and a separately supplied final trust bundle.
